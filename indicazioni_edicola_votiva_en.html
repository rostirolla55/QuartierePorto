How to Reach the Votive Shrine

Starting from Piazzetta della Pioggia (located at the intersection of Via Galliera and Via di Riva di Reno, next to the Church of Santa Maria della Pioggia), the short walk takes just a few steps:

  • Walk straight onto Via Avesella, following its straight path.
  • After a very short walk, turn onto the first side street on the left: Via Tanari Vecchia.
  • The votive shrine depicting the Madonna and Child is located right on the corner of Via Avesella and Via Tanari Vecchia.
Note for visitors: Although some digital maps show a slight curve at this junction, on the ground Via Avesella is perfectly straight from the square up to the first left turn.
