Visitor Tip

When standing before the Madonna and Child votive shrine at the corner of Via Tanari and Via Avesella, take a few steps into the small square and enter the Sanctuary of Santa Maria della Pioggia on your right: it houses valuable artworks and the miraculous image tied to the history of this street.
