Current Status and Post-Earthquake Consolidation (2012-2026)

  • Earthquake Damage (2012): Following the tremors of May 2012, the church suffered structural damage that compromised its usability, leading to its prolonged closure. For years, parish services were transferred to the nearby parish of San Benedetto on Via Indipendenza.

  • Safety Restoration and Special Reopenings: In recent years, the entire complex has undergone delicate structural consolidation and conservative restoration work, aimed at allowing its gradual reopening for guided tours and special cultural events.

Anecdotes, Curiosities and Historical Insights

  1. The Engineering Challenge of Consolidation: The "hybrid" nature of the complex made the earthquake-proofing work a unique case study: the engineers had to consolidate the 1950s reinforced concrete structure without transmitting vibrations or stresses that could crack the delicate 17th-century frescoes on the vault of the adjacent oratory.

  2. The Name "San Carlo al Porto": The church's name recalls its proximity to Bologna's old Porto Navile , a point of arrival for merchants and waterborne transport from the plains. The brotherhood offered spiritual comfort to sailors, fishermen, and dockworkers.

  3. Sant'Antonio "Neighbor": The strong devotion to Sant'Antonio in this area is linked to the Saint's stay in 1222 at the convent of Bernardo di Quintavalle in nearby Abbadia , a memory that remained alive among the inhabitants of the Porto district for centuries.
