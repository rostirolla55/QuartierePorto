X- Lam technology (also known as CLT , from the English Cross Laminated Timber ) represents one of the most significant innovations in the field of sustainable construction. It is a construction system based on cross-laminated solid wood panels.

How is an X- Lam panel made ?

An X- Lam panel is composed of several layers (usually 3, 5, or 7) of dried coniferous wood (often fir) boards, stacked so that the grain of each layer is rotated 90° with respect to the adjacent one. The layers are then glued together under strong pressure.

This "crossed" configuration gives the material exceptional properties:

  • Dimensional stability: Variations in wood due to humidity are cancelled out by the orthogonal arrangement of the layers.

  • Mechanical resistance: The panel can withstand very high loads, behaving like a rigid "plate", similar to reinforced concrete but much lighter.
