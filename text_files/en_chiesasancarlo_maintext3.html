Certified Source

The church of San Carlo Borromeo al Porto stands on the site of the ancient "Crocetta", built in 1619 to a design by Nicolò Barelli and expanded in the 18th century.

Bologna Online / Salaborsa Library / BeWeB CEI

On January 29, 1944, an Allied bombing raid almost entirely destroyed the main church, miraculously leaving the Baroque Oratory from 1667 on the upper floor intact.

History and Memory of Bologna / Historical Archives of the Archdiocese of Bologna

The modern building was rebuilt in the 1950s based on a design by architect Alfredo Cosentino.

Dictionary of 20th-Century Bolognese Architects / Origins of Bologna

Following the 2012 earthquake, the church suffered structural damage, which led to its closure, with services being transferred to San Benedetto and subsequent restoration.

Cultural Heritage Office of the Archdiocese of Bologna / Post-Earthquake Restoration Documentation
