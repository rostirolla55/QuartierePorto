The address Via San Carlo, 19 in Bologna is associated with a building historically referred to as "Casa Carracci" (currently also known as "Casa Vacanze San Carlo" and used as tourist accommodation).

The building, protected by the Superintendence for Cultural Heritage, is identified by local tradition as one of the places where the painter Ludovico Carracci (Bologna, 1555 – Bologna, 1619) lived and worked, as well as one of the early workshops of the three Carracci cousins (Ludovico, Agostino, and Annibale), who founded the Accademia dei Desiderosi (later known as the Accademia degli Incamminati) in 1582.
